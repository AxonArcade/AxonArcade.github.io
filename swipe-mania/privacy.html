Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

In short: Swipe Mania does not collect, send or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not connect to the internet. Everything the game saves stays on your phone.

Who we are

Swipe Mania is made by Axon Arcade. This policy covers the Swipe Mania app.

What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps these files in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Your best score, best streak and the number of runs you have played.
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, reduced motion and relaxed mode.
  • A short log of each run: how long it lasted, your score, the level you reached and how the run ended. It helps us balance the game during testing.
  • The score picture, if you use Share score.

None of this leaves your phone. It does not include your name, contacts, location or anything else about you. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes all of it.

What the game does not do

  • It does not ask for an account, email address or any other personal information.
  • It does not show ads and does not include advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not request internet access, so it cannot send anything to us or to anyone else.

Sharing your score

On the Run Over screen you can tap Share score. The game makes a picture of your score and opens your phone's share menu. You choose which app gets it, or cancel. The game sends nothing on its own; whatever app you share to handles the picture under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for short vibrations when you swipe. This is the only permission the game asks for.

Children

Swipe Mania does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what it collects, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us through Axon Arcade on GitHub.
