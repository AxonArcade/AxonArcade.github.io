Swipe Mania

Swipe by the rule.
The rule keeps changing.

A card appears and tells you which way to swipe. Get it right before the timer runs out. New levels unlock as you go, the game keeps mixing them, and it keeps speeding up.

How to play

Read. Swipe. Don't blink.

  1. Read the card

    It shows a word, a colour or a shape. The rule above the card tells you what it means right now.

  2. Swipe before the bar runs out

    A wrong swipe or running out of time costs a heart. The card stays until you get it right. Three hearts and the run is over.

  3. Keep the streak going

    Streaks multiply your points: ×2 at 10 in a row, ×3 at 25. The first card after a rule change is a free pass.

9 levels

A new rule every 14 cards

Each level adds a new rule. Once you've reached it, it joins the mix, so later in a run you never know which rule comes next.

  1. LEFT
    Level 1

    Swipe the way the WORD says

    LEFT = swipe left

  2. NOT
    UP
    Level 2

    NOT means any OTHER way

    NOT UP = left, right or down

  3. BLUE
    Level 3

    Swipe to the MATCHING colour

    Red card = red edge

  4. ORANGE
    Level 4

    More colours. The edges move!

    Find the card's colour on an edge

  5. NOT
    RED
    Level 5

    NOT means any OTHER edge

    NOT RED = any edge but red

  6. ●▲■★ STAR
    Level 6

    Swipe to the MATCHING shape

    STAR = the star edge

  7. ●▲■★ NOT
    STAR
    Level 7

    NOT means any OTHER shape

    NOT STAR = any edge but the star

  8. PINK
    Level 8

    Go by the WORD, not the colour

    BLUE on a red card = blue edge

  9. ★■●▲ SQUARE
    Level 9

    Go by the SHAPE, not the colour

    Red CIRCLE = the circle edge, any colour
