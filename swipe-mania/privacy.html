Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: Swipe Mania has no accounts and keeps your scores and settings on your phone. It shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google's ad software collects some information from your device to do that, such as your advertising ID and IP address. Where the law requires it, the game asks for your choice before any ads load, and you can change it later in Settings.

Who we are

Swipe Mania is made by Axon Arcade. This policy covers the Swipe Mania app.

What the game saves on your device

To remember your progress, the game keeps these files in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Your best score, best streak and the number of runs you have played.
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, reduced motion and relaxed mode.
  • A short log of each run: how long it lasted, your score, the level you reached and how the run ended. It helps us balance the game during testing.
  • The score picture, if you use Share score.

The game does not send these files to us, to Google or to anyone else. They do not include your name, contacts or location. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in your phone's settings, deletes them.

Ads

Swipe Mania shows ads served by Google AdMob. You see them in two places:

  • An extra heart, if you want one. When you lose your last heart, you can choose to watch a short ad to keep going. If you don't, the run simply ends.
  • Between runs. An ad may show when you leave the Run Over screen. You won't get one if you just watched an ad for an extra heart.

To show and measure these ads, Google's ad software in the app collects information from your device, which can include:

  • your device's IP address, which can show your approximate location
  • your device's advertising ID and other app and device identifiers
  • how you interact with the ads, such as views and taps
  • technical and diagnostic information, such as device model, operating system and crash and performance data

Google uses this to deliver ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, show ads based on your interests. Google is responsible for this data. Read how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not get this personal information from Google. AdMob only shows us totals, such as how many ads were shown and what they earned.

Your choices

  • Consent. Where privacy law requires it, for example in the European Economic Area and the UK, the game shows Google's consent form when it starts, and no ads load until you've made your choice. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy. The button only appears where the choice applies.
  • Advertising ID. In your phone's settings (on most Android phones, Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads) you can reset or delete your advertising ID. Once it is deleted, apps can no longer use it to personalise ads.
  • Playing offline. The game works without an internet connection. Without one, no ads load and no information goes to Google.

Sharing your score

On the Run Over screen you can tap Share score. The game makes a picture of your score and opens your phone's share menu. You choose which app gets it, or cancel. The game sends nothing on its own; whatever app you share to handles the picture under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: lets Google's ad software read your device's advertising ID.
  • Vibrate: for short vibrations when you swipe.
  • Google's ad software also adds a few technical permissions it needs to run, such as keeping the device awake briefly and reading the network type.

The game cannot access your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.

Children

Swipe Mania is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game changes what it collects or who it shares information with, we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released.
